Moscow Mule

Description

A Moscow mule is a vodka-based cocktail made with lime juice and ginger beer.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 fluid ounces vodka
  • 0.5 fluid oz lime juice
  • Ice Cubes
  • 0.5 cup Ginger Beer
  • 1 lime wedge for garnish

Steps

  1. Gather the ingredients.
  2. Pour vodka and lime juice into a copper mug.
  3. Add ginger beer.
  4. Add ice.
  5. Stir to combine.
  6. Drop a lime wedge into the mug for garnish.
