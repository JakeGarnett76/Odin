Arnold Palmer

Description

Arnold Palmers is the best answer to a hot summer afternoon. Beat the heat with a ready batch of sweet tea, lemonade, and bourbon to serve over ice waiting in the refrigerator.

Ingredients

  • 7 cups sweet tea
  • 6 cups lemonade
  • 3 cups bourbon whiskey

Steps

  1. Combine sweet tea, lemonade, and bourbon in a 1-gallon jug.
  2. Seal the jug and carefully tilt it upside down a couple time to mix the ingredients.
  3. Refrigerate until ready to serve; serve over ice.
