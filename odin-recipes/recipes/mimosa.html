Mimosa

Description

A mimosa is a 2-ingredient cocktail made with fresh orange juice and Champagne (or sparkling wine) that's commonly served at brunch or at daytime celebrations.

Ingredients

  • 0.25 cup orange juice, chilled
  • 0.75 cup Champagne or sparkling wine, chilled

Steps

  1. Gather the ingredients.
  2. Divide orange juice evenly into two flute glasses.
  3. Top with chilled Champagne or sparkling wine.
